Three steps, one happy kid

Each sheet has a little picture of what you're drawing, letter boxes for your child's name, and its own QR code. Every page is made for black-and-white printers.

1

Start at 1 and follow the numbers. Easy sheets have 30 dots, the trickiest has 73, so there's always a next challenge.

2

Crayons, markers or pencils all work. The little colored picture on the page shows one way to do it.

3

Scan and watch

Point the phone camera at the QR code, open the link, then aim it at the page. The picture starts to move.

Your child's own colors come alive

The magic camera reads the colors your child used and turns their drawing into a little dancing character. You can record it and send it to grandma. It opens as a web page, so there's nothing to download.

  • No appScan the QR code with the normal camera on an iPhone or Android phone.
  • No accounts or emailsThe AR page doesn't ask for names, emails or logins, so it's fine for little ones.
  • One code per sheetEvery picture has its own animation, matched to that drawing.
  • Says hello by nameIf the camera can read the name in the letter boxes, the character says "Hi Susan!". If not, it simply dances.
  • Record and shareSave a short video of the dance to your phone, or share it with family.

Questions parents ask

Will anything be posted to me?

No. You get two PDF files, one for A4 paper and one for US Letter, to download from Etsy right after you buy. Print them at home as often as you like.

Do we need to install an app?

No. Scan the QR code with the phone's camera and the animation opens in the web browser.

What age are the sheets for?

Ages 4 to 8. The easiest pages have 30 dots and the hardest has 73. Every page shows difficulty stars so you can pick the right one.

Do I need a color printer?

No. Every page is designed for black-and-white printing. The colors come from your child. There's one optional "color ideas" page at the end that looks best in color.

How should I print them?

Print at 100% or "Actual size" so the QR code and the page print at the right size. Plain white paper works fine.

What happens to my child's name?

The magic camera reads the name on your phone and forgets it when you close the page. It isn't uploaded or saved anywhere.

Can I use them in my classroom?

Yes, please message us on Etsy first and we'll sort out classroom use for your group.

Who makes the pictures?

MaryMariiArt designs every bundle. Some illustrations are made with the help of AI tools, and each Etsy listing says so.
