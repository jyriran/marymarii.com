Questions

How do I get the files?

Right after paying you get a download link on screen and by email. Download on a computer for easiest printing.

Do I need an app?

No. Scan the QR code with the normal phone camera and the magic camera opens in the browser.

Which printer and paper?

Any home printer, black and white is fine. Plain A4 or US Letter paper works; thicker paper is nicer for crayons and markers.

What age is it for?

Ages 3–8. Grown-ups read the clues to little ones; the checklists and bingo use pictures.

Color or black and white?

Both work. The bingo cards come in both versions.

Can I use it in my classroom?

Yes, you can print for your own class. For a whole school, send us a message at info@marymarii.com.
